About Magnolia

Designed once. Built with intention.

Magnolia Creations is a preset architectural studio specializing in thoughtfully designed Bloxburg homes. Every build is designed by me, refined through personal iteration, and offered as a finished layout rather than a commission.

Instead of custom requests, Magnolia focuses on a curated collection of pre-built designs that can be placed directly onto your lot — clean, efficient, and architecturally intentional.

How It Works

• Choose a preset design from our listings
• Purchase the layout for a small fixed fee
• The build is placed directly on your lot using your in-game budget
• No commissions, revisions, or structural changes

This approach allows for consistent quality, faster turnaround, and designs that are fully resolved before they ever reach your plot.
